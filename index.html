What the queue is telling you

Mezo is a Liquity-style CDP: MUSD is redeemable for $1 of BTC against the lowest-ICR trove above the 110% MCR. A healthy-looking borrower can therefore still lose collateral. The radar plots every trove in that queue, so a position is only ever described in the one unit that matters — how much redemption volume stands between it and the tail.

Why it is atomic

Loops, unwinds and exits all borrow MUSD from a Tigris flash pool, act on the trove, and repay the pool inside one transaction. That is what removes the up-front capital requirement: nothing needs to be sitting in a wallet, and a failure costs gas instead of a half-finished position.

What stays yours

Pylon never holds collateral, never takes custody and cannot move a trove without an EIP-712 authorisation signed by its owner. The engine service can relay a transaction, but it cannot change its contents: the same struct is verified on chain against the signature.
