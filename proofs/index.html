How to verify a row
  1. Open the transaction hash on explorer.test.mezo.org.
  2. For a loop: the log shows Looped(owner, musdBorrowed, btcAdded, feeMusd) — one transaction, one flash borrow, one swap, one trove adjustment.
  3. For an unwind: Unwound(owner, musdRepaid, btcWithdrawn, btcSwapped, feeMusd) with zero MUSD leaving the owner's wallet.
  4. For a shield: Defended(musdRepaid, preICR, postICR, reserveDebt) on the position.

Instrument notes
  • Pylon is non-custodial: nothing is held between transactions.
  • Flash liquidity is borrowed and repaid inside a single transaction.
  • Runs on Mezo Testnet only. Amounts are testnet MUSD and BTC.
  • Engine v2 is live on 31611; v1 was superseded after a repayment-accounting fault.
