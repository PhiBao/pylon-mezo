The trap

A Mezo loan is static, and the queue is invisible.

  1. Looping is manual. Borrow → swap → re-collateralise, 6–15 transactions, each moving your ratio.
  2. Repayment needs MUSD you may not have. Collateral is released only after the repayment. A borrower with equity and a falling price cannot reach it.
  3. Liquidation is not the only risk. Redemptions walk up from the lowest ICR above 110%. A 135% borrower loses BTC with no liquidation warning.

429M MUSD of redemption depth behind 296 testnet troves — and no tooling to steer around it.

The insight

Mezo already contains every part. Nobody composed them.

BorrowerOperationsSignatures

A contract can act on your existing trove from one EIP-712 signature. No custody, no migration.

Tigris pools

Solidly-style hook flash liquidity, inside the DEX you already trade on.

The BTC ledger

One 18-decimal native/ERC-20 ledger, so BTC bought in a swap pays the next call's value directly.

That composition is the product: three atomic paths and a priced defence market, built on primitives Mezo already ships.

innovation03 / 10
What it does

Four operations. One transaction each.

ModeWhat happens
Flash LoopBorrow, swap to BTC, post it as collateral, repay the flash out of the trove's new borrow.
Flash UnwindRepay debt out of the trove's own collateral — with zero MUSD in the wallet.
Flash ExitClose the trove entirely, funded by itself, and return the rest as BTC.
Redemption ShieldA MUSD reserve that only ever repays a shielded position's debt, within hard caps, with the claim enforced in code.

The signature carries the exact collateral and debt deltas. If a swap cannot deliver them, the whole transaction reverts.

evidence05 / 10
Why this is hard

Constraints only live infrastructure reveals.

  1. A Solidly pool cannot be swapped inside its own flash callback — two venues, by design.
  2. The signature helper burns from its own caller — keep the flash proceeds in the engine.
  3. The BTC ledger is one balance — value is already credited when the callee body runs.
  4. Hint resolution is expensive — the solver supplies hints, the contract validates them.

We found these by executing them — then had twelve adversarial lenses look for what we missed: 35 findings, 32 fixed, each with a regression test.

engineering06 / 10
The shield economy

A priced, enforceable market for redemption defence.

  1. Borrowers arm a band: defend below X% ICR, toward Y%.
  2. Anyone can defend — the owner, a keeper, a competing bot.
  3. The reserve lends MUSD that can only repay that position's debt.
  4. The claim blocks collateral release until it is settled.

track record08 / 10
Beyond the hackathon

The MUSD economy is the market.

Mezo mainnet lists 33 MUSD pairs — BTC, mUSDC, mUSDT, WUSD, sUSDat, mSolvBTC, and the tokenised-equity pairs (MSTR, COIN, TSLA, NVDA, GOOGL, CRCL, BTDR) that make Mezo different. Every one has the same redemption risk and no equivalent tool.

The engine takes its venues as constructor arguments, so serving a new pair is a deployment, not a rewrite. Next: defence ballots for native troves, multi-leg yield routing, a keeper bounty market, and an external audit before third-party capital.
